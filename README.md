# 🚑 LIFEWAY AI

## Intelligent Emergency Green Corridor System

**LIFEWAY AI** is an AI-powered emergency traffic management system designed to help ambulances, fire engines, and police vehicles move through congested urban roads with minimum delay.

### 🌐 Live Prototype

👉 https://tireless-clear-path-live.base44.app/command

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Emergency vehicles often face traffic congestion, long signal waiting times, blocked roads, and inefficient traffic coordination. These delays can affect emergency response.

Traditional traffic signals generally operate independently and may not prepare a complete route for an approaching emergency vehicle.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

LIFEWAY AI detects emergency vehicles, analyzes traffic conditions, predicts congestion, finds an efficient route, and coordinates multiple traffic signals to create a **Green Emergency Corridor**.

### 🔄 Core Workflow

**DETECT → ANALYZE → PREDICT → OPTIMIZE → CONTROL → MEASURE**

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. The system detects an emergency vehicle.
2. It identifies the vehicle's current location.
3. Traffic density is analyzed.
4. Possible emergency routes are evaluated.
5. AI selects an efficient route.
6. Traffic signals along the route are coordinated.
7. A Green Emergency Corridor is created.
8. The emergency vehicle is monitored in real time.
9. If congestion changes, the route can be recalculated.
10. The system records performance metrics.

---

## 🚦 Green Corridor

The main concept of LIFEWAY AI is to prepare the road **before the emergency vehicle reaches each intersection**.

Instead of controlling only one traffic signal, LIFEWAY AI coordinates multiple signals along the emergency route.

**Emergency Vehicle → Junction 1 🟢 → Junction 2 🟢 → Junction 3 🟢 → Hospital**
